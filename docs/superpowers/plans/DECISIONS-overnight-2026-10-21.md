# Overnight decisions for the owner's review (2026-10-20 → 21)

The owner handed over overnight: decide, record, and keep working; stop only for a decision that can't be taken without them.

Every decision below was weighed against the owner's three principles:
1. **Windows and Mac:** the framework serves a 1000-person org whose developers use Windows and Mac laptops (CI is Linux).
2. **Provider-agnostic:** no dependency on one model provider or LLM.
3. **Quality and user experience** come first.

Each row reads left to right: the problem, the alternatives, the decision taken, and why, plus what it costs if the decision is wrong. Items still open for the owner are in DEFERRED.md marked **Needs decision**.
